# Siteline

> Siteline is billing and accounts receivable software built specifically for commercial subcontractors (trade contractors). It replaces the spreadsheets, PDFs, and GC payment portal logins that AP/AR teams juggle every month, centralizing pay applications, lien waivers, lien rights, compliance documents, change orders, collections, and A/R reporting in one workflow. Siteline integrates with most construction accounting systems (Sage, Foundation, Viewpoint/Vista, Spectrum, QuickBooks, Acumatica, and others) and with GC payment portals (Textura, GCPay, Procore Pay, etc.), so billing data flows between them without double entry. Customers typically get paid about three weeks faster, reduce DSO by ~30%, and bill roughly 6x faster than they did before. Siteline was founded in 2019 by Claire Wilson (CEO, former Tishman Construction PM) and Gloria Lin (President, former Stripe/Apple PM). To date, subcontractors have billed over $10B across 200,000+ projects through the platform. The product is priced per subscription (demo required for pricing), sold in the U.S., and is not a construction accounting system itself — it sits on top of one and handles everything downstream of job costing that a controller, A/R manager, or PM has to do to actually collect the money.

## Features (what the software actually does)

- [Pay App Management](https://www.siteline.com/feature/construction-payment-application-software): Generate and submit pay applications — including AIA G702/G703-style forms and any GC-specific custom form — in under 5 minutes, with digital signatures. Siteline has digitized 23,000+ pay app and lien waiver forms from 17,000+ general contractors.
- [AIA Billing Software](https://www.siteline.com/feature/aia-billing-software): Purpose-built for AIA® G702/G703 progress billing (Application and Certificate for Payment + Continuation Sheet).
- [Lien Waiver Management](https://www.siteline.com/feature/lien-waiver-management-software): Automated generation, collection, and submission of conditional/unconditional progress and final lien waivers.
- [Lien Rights Management](https://www.siteline.com/feature/lien-rights-management-software): Tracks state-specific preliminary notice and mechanic's lien deadlines, generates required notices, and manages certified delivery so subs preserve their right to file a lien if they aren't paid.
- [Vendor Management](https://www.siteline.com/feature/construction-vendor-management-software): Collection of lien waivers and compliance documents from lower-tier vendors and sub-subs (the documents the sub has to gather before the GC will release payment).
- [Compliance Tracking](https://www.siteline.com/feature/construction-compliance-document-tracking-software): Monitors certificates of insurance, W-9s, and other compliance docs so payments aren't held up for missing or expired paperwork.
- [Change Order Tracking](https://www.siteline.com/feature/construction-change-order-tracking-software): Centralized log of pending, approved, and work-in-progress change orders, with flow-through to the SOV once approved.
- [A/R Reporting](https://www.siteline.com/feature/accounts-receivable-reporting-software): Real-time aging, billed-vs-collected, and which GCs pay fastest/slowest.
- [Billing and Cash Flow Forecasting](https://www.siteline.com/feature/billing-and-cash-flow-forecasting-software): Projected billing and cash receipts across the project backlog.
- [Collections Management](https://www.siteline.com/feature/collections-management): Task assignments, scheduled payment reminders to GCs, and activity tracking to reduce invoice aging.
